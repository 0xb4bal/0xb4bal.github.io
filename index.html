03 / INVESTIGATION WORKFLOW

From signal to assessment.

Six steps. One principle: make every conclusion traceable to the evidence.

01

Frame the alert

Start with the behaviour the alert claims to detect. Identify the source, time window, affected asset, and the condition that triggered it.

A clear investigation question.

Alert contextEntityTime window
  1. Frame the alert. Start with the behaviour the alert claims to detect. Identify the source, time window, affected asset, and the condition that triggered it.
  2. Validate the telemetry. Compare parsed fields with the raw event. Check timestamps, field meaning, and gaps before treating a log entry as evidence.
  3. Connect the evidence. Search historical activity and pivot across IPs, accounts, hosts, and related events. Establish whether the behaviour is isolated, repeated, or part of a wider pattern.
  4. Enrich with context. Use threat intelligence to support a hypothesis. Consider shared infrastructure, source recency, and disagreement between providers before drawing a conclusion.
  5. Assess the outcome. Distinguish an attempted action from a successful one. Interpret security-control responses in context, state confidence, and make remaining uncertainty explicit.
  6. Document and hand off. Write down observations, assessment, uncertainty, potential impact, and the recommended next action. Give the next analyst enough context to continue.
